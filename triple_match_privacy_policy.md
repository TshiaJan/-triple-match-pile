# Privacy Policy for Triple Match Pile

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026
email janaire.dev@gmail.com

Thank you for playing **Triple Match Pile** ("we", "our", or "the Game"). We value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled when you enjoy our puzzle game on mobile, tablet, or web devices.

---

## 1. Core Principles
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, phone number, location, or contact list.
- **No Account Required:** You can play every endless level without registration, login, or social authentication.
- **Virtual Pretend Tokens:** All tokens and stars referenced in the game are pretend points used exclusively for replay progression; they carry zero real-world monetary or trade value.
- **Local Storage Only:** Game progress (levels beaten, high scores, token counts, and accessibility settings) is saved entirely on your device via standard browser or webview storage (`localStorage`).
- **Offline Playability:** The hand-written physics simulation and level generator function fully offline without requiring an active internet connection.

---

## 2. Information We Store On-Device
To remember your game state across play sessions, the Game retains the following keys locally on your device:
- **Gameplay Progress:** Current level number, best level reached, virtual tokens, and stars collected.
- **Player Preferences:** Sound effects toggle, big-ball / big-text accessibility toggle, and hint overlays.

This information remains strictly confined to your local hardware and is never transmitted to external developer servers.

---

## 3. Advertising & Analytics (If Applicable)
If optional rewarded video advertisements or non-personalized banner ads are activated in a store release:
- Third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads) may collect non-personal device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse regional IP location, and ad performance interaction metrics.
- These networks operate according to their respective privacy terms. You can reset or opt out of ad tracking at any time through your device's operating system settings (Settings > Google > Ads on Android, or Settings > Privacy > Tracking on iOS).

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
Triple Match Pile is a family-friendly game designed for audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

---

## 5. Data Control & Deletion
Because your game data lives exclusively on your local device:
- You can clear your game data anytime by clearing your app or browser storage.
- Uninstalling the Game permanently deletes all local gameplay history and saved preferences.
